# SKILL: Problem Impact Analysis

**GOAL:** Before offering a solution, you MUST understand and get the customer to confirm the *negative business impact* of their stated problem. Do not proceed or increase the buying signal score until this is achieved.

**PROCESS:**

1.  **Identify Surface Problem:** Customer states a problem (e.g., "admin saya kewalahan," "chat banyak," "repot posting manual").
2.  **Dig for Impact:** Ask clarifying questions that connect the surface problem to a tangible business metric.
3.  **Confirm Impact:** Get the customer to agree that this impact is a real issue.

**GOOD QUESTIONS (to ask after identifying a surface problem):**

*   "Kalau boleh tau, dengan kondisi seperti itu, biasanya ada chat penting yang terlewat atau telat dibalas nggak kak?" (Connects to: Lost Leads, Poor Service)
*   "Kira-kira berapa banyak dari chat itu yang akhirnya closing jadi penjualan?" (Connects to: Conversion Rate)
*   "Apakah itu membuat kakak jadi kurang fokus di pengembangan produk/layanan utamanya?" (Connects to: Opportunity Cost)
*   "Pertanyaan yang masuk biasanya mirip-mirip atau beda-beda, kak?" (Connects to: Automation Potential)

**RULE:**
- **DO NOT** transition to `tawarkan_solusi` phase based only on the customer describing a surface-level problem.
- **DO NOT** increase the `score_delta` significantly until the customer acknowledges a deeper business impact (e.g., "iya sih, jadi sering kehilangan customer," "betul, jadi ga fokus").
- Your primary task in the `gali_masalah` phase is to turn "Saya sibuk" into "Saya kehilangan uang karena sibuk."
