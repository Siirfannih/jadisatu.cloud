# SKILL: Social Proof & Case Studies

**GOAL:** When presenting a solution, increase trust and reduce perceived risk by providing relevant, concrete examples of success.

**TRIGGERS:**
- After explaining how a feature works (e.g., "AI kami bisa balas chat otomatis").
- When the customer seems hesitant or asks, "Apakah ini benar-benar berfungsi?"
- During the `tawarkan_solusi` phase.

**PROCESS:**

1.  **Listen to Customer's Business Type:** Are they in F&B, fashion, digital services, etc.?
2.  **Select a Relevant Case Study:** Choose an anonymous but specific example from the knowledge base.
3.  **Frame the Story:** Use the "Problem-Solution-Result" framework.
4.  **Keep it Brief:** One or two sentences is enough.

**EXAMPLES:**

*   **For F&B:** "Kami ada handle klien katering, kak. Dulu mereka sering kehilangan orderan di jam sibuk. Setelah pakai AI, orderan yang masuk via WA naik 30% karena semua langsung terespon."
*   **For Fashion/Retail:** "Mirip seperti salah satu klien kami di bidang fashion. Dulu adminnya sering salah kasih info stok. Sekarang AI yang handle, jadi komplain soal stok salah hampir nol."
*   **For Digital Services/Creators:** "Betul kak. Ada kreator yang kami bantu, dia bisa hemat sekitar 2 jam per hari yang biasanya habis cuma buat jawab pertanyaan berulang. Waktunya jadi bisa buat bikin konten."

**RULE:**
- **DO NOT** use generic claims like "banyak klien kami sukses."
- **DO** use specific, (anonymized) stories with quantifiable results if possible.
- Tie the story directly to the pain point the customer just confirmed.
